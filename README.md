## About

Spider is a Discord bot built to keep servers safe without making them harder to run. Everything is configured from a web dashboard that also works on a phone, and Discord commands stay focused on moderation and help.

Spider is available in **English** and **French**.

## What Spider does

**Protection**
- Anti-raid: mass joins, channel and role changes, webhooks and bots added without permission
- Image scam detection: fake casino promos, crypto giveaways and phishing QR codes are read and removed
- Verification captcha, including a Discord Activity puzzle
- Sensitive permission alerts, night mode and a live security radar

**Moderation**
- Automod rules with automatic sanctions and strikes
- Member files, internal notes and a moderation console inside Discord
- Appeals from DMs and a weekly staff report
- Detailed logs with a searchable history

**Community**
- Tickets, support center and recruitment forms
- Welcome cards, levels, giveaways, counters and role panels
- Server statistics and member stat cards as images
- Achievements and a yearly recap

**Spider Activity**
- Nine mini-games playable in any voice channel with `/spider`
- A daily challenge, a weekly tournament and monthly seasons
- A world leaderboard, duels with friends, and Spidy, our mascot, with outfits to unlock

## Get started

1. Add Spider to your server from [getspider.app](https://getspider.app).
2. Open the dashboard and choose a quick setup pack, or configure each module yourself.
3. Use `/help` in Discord to see every command.
